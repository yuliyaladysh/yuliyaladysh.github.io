Chapter 1

One scholarship, four universities

It started in Minsk with a degree in International Business Communication, which, looking back, was a very accurate spoiler for everything that followed. Then came a master’s in Poland and an MBA in Costa Rica.

And then a scholarship to study in Switzerland. That’s the honest reason my academic path has four universities on it: not indecision, just one door opening onto the next.

Chapter 3

Plot twist: 2020

Then the world closed its stadiums.

With events on pause, I had to reinvent myself. So I learnt two programming languages, and that’s how my tech journey began: testing software, then designing it.

Since then I’ve tested and designed apps at Bending Spoons, including Filmic and Firstlight, done a UX/UI bootcamp at Ironhack, and worked as a Software QA Engineer at ABC Fitness.

Chapter 4 · Now

Making small businesses run themselves (a bit)

Since January 2026 I’ve been working as a freelance AI Transformation Consultant under All Sorted HQ. I help small and growing businesses in tourism, travel and import/export replace manual work with AI-powered operations: enquiries, quotes, bookings, suppliers, invoicing and follow-ups. Then I train the team, so the new tools actually stick.
